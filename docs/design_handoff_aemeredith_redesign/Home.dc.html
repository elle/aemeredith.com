02

Elle is a well-rounded executive consultant and leadership coach with a background in software engineering. Her strengths are asking difficult questions while focusing on the bigger picture and business goals, growing high performing teams and leaders, and communicating with all relevant stakeholders.

Elle is passionate about creating an environment that brings out the best in people. While she is an accomplished technologist, she believes in balancing business results with people retention. She leads with curiosity, understanding the “why” gives her the context to lead with integrity.

Elle developed and ran two six-month engineering academies for Qantas Hotels and Disco. The curriculum covered programming fundamentals, clean code, refactoring, and agile workflows. She was on the Victorian Government’s Industry Advisory Panel to help assess digital skills training courses for the new Digital Skills and Jobs Program.

In 2021, she completed a post-grad certificate in Psychology for Business and Management at Curtin University. In 2025, she started her Masters in Leadership and Management (organisation dynamics) with NIODA.

She believes in writing clean code, driven by automatic tests, with agile practices, an even work/life balance, and a respectful and inclusive team culture. Her aim is to deliver clean, functional and user‑friendly applications in an appropriate timeframe without wasting resources.

When she is not immersed in the Ruby community, she is probably immersed in water, or lately, in bread flour.

03

Community

Elle has a passion for helping people learn to work better. She appreciates the value of strong communities. In 2014, she organised Rails Girls events in Sydney, RubyConfAU 2014, and was a member of the Ruby Australia committee. In 2015, she co-organised Gotham Ruby Conference (GORUCO) in New York City, and started a monthly work jelly for women in tech. In 2016, she co-organised Rails Camp USA East Coast in the Catskills, NY and once again co-organised GORUCO X. In 2018, she helped with the last GORUCO conference.
